- **fix(qoder):** `qodercli` runs now go through a process-wide FIFO queue (`QODER_CLI_MAX_CONCURRENCY`, default 2; `QODER_CLI_QUEUE_TIMEOUT_MS` → 503 `qodercli_busy`), and timeout or abort kills the whole process group, so a burst of `qoder/*` requests no longer spawns unbounded ~780 MB CLI processes and OOMs the host; an experimental opt-in persistent ACP transport (`QODER_CLI_TRANSPORT=acp`) is also available ([#17004](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/pull/17004)) — thanks @mad-gooze
