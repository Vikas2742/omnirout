```bash
POST /v1/chat/completions
Authorization: Bearer your-api-key
Content-Type: application/json

{
  "model": "cc/claude-opus-4-6",
  "messages": [
    {"role": "user", "content": "Write a function to..."}

  "stream": true
```

#### Ɗaure asusu ga kowace buƙata

Idan provider yana da connections da yawa (asusun ko maɓallan shiga), OmniRoute yawanci yana zaɓar ɗaya
ta hanyar dabarar zaɓen asusu. Maimakon haka, buƙata za ta iya ɗaure takamaiman connection guda ɗaya
ta amfani da header na `x-omniroute-connection`:

```bash
curl http://localhost:20128/v1/chat/completions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer <your OmniRoute API key>" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "x-omniroute-connection: <connection id>" \
  -d '{"model": "codex/gpt-5.5", "messages": [{"role": "user", "content": "hi"}]}'
```

- **Inda za a sami id ɗin.** Buɗe shafin provider a dashboard
  (**Dashboard → Providers → <provider>**) sannan danna guntun id na `xxxxxxxx…` a layin connection
  — hakan yana kwafe cikakken connection id.
- **Buƙatun da ke mutunta shi.** Buƙatun model guda ɗaya (`provider/model`) a
  `/v1/chat/completions`, `/v1/messages` da `/v1/responses`. Combos ba sa la'akari da header ɗin: a
  maimakon haka, ɗaure asusu ga kowane matakin combo ta hanyar connection na matakin da kansa.
- **Ɗaurin tsauri ne, ba fifiko kawai ba.** Ana amfani da connection da aka ɗaure shi kaɗai. Idan
  matsala ta faru a cikin buƙatar, za a rubuta lokacin cooldown na asusun amma **ba** za a koma zuwa
  wani asusun provider ɗin ba.
- **Cooldown da quota suna s解除 ɗaurin.** Yayin da connection da aka ɗaure yake cikin cooldown,
  quota ta ƙare ko wata manufar quota ta hana shi, buƙatu na gaba za su koma zuwa sauran connections na provider,
  daidai kamar traffic da ba a ɗaure ba.
- **Ids da ba a sani ba ko aka kashe suna haifar da gazawa.** Id da ba ya cikin active pool na provider (an share shi,
  an kashe shi, ko yana na wani provider) ba ya barin wani asusu da ya cancanta, don haka buƙatar
  za ta gaza maimakon a tura ta wani wuri a ɓoye.

Claude Code yana aika custom headers ta hanyar `ANTHROPIC_CUSTOM_HEADERS`. Saka shi a cikin block na `env`
na `~/.claude/settings.json`, ko a cikin `.claude/settings.json` na matakin project domin ɗaure wani
asusu daban ga kowane project:

```jsonc
{
  "env": {
    "ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL": "http://<your OmniRoute>:20128",
    "ANTHROPIC_AUTH_TOKEN": "<your OmniRoute API key>",
    "ANTHROPIC_CUSTOM_HEADERS": "x-omniroute-connection: <connection id>",
  },
}
```

Madogara: `src/sse/handlers/chat.ts` (karanta header), `src/sse/services/auth.ts` da
`src/sse/services/sessionAffinityPin.ts` (s解除 ɗauri da binciken rashin kasancewa a pool).

> **Headers na bayanan telemetry na kuɗi:** martanin nasara marar streaming kuma yana ɗauke da saitin telemetry na kuɗi na `X-OmniRoute-*` — `X-OmniRoute-Response-Cost` (USD, lambobi 10 tabbatattu bayan aya; `0.0000000000` ga abin da yake kyauta/marar farashi), `X-OmniRoute-Tokens-In` / `X-OmniRoute-Tokens-Out`, `X-OmniRoute-Model`, `X-OmniRoute-Provider`, `X-OmniRoute-Latency-Ms`, `X-OmniRoute-Cache-Hit`, da `X-OmniRoute-Fallback-Attempts` (kawai idan > 0), tare da `X-OmniRoute-Request-Id` da `X-OmniRoute-Version`. Chat completions, `/v1/responses`, `/v1/messages`, **da media endpoints** ne suke fitar da waɗannan — `/v1/embeddings`, `/v1/images/generations`, `/v1/audio/speech`, `/v1/audio/transcriptions`, `/v1/rerank`, `/v1/videos/generations`, `/v1/music/generations`, da `/v1/moderations` (kullum kuɗinsa `0`). Ana ƙididdige kuɗin media bisa kowane modality (ga kowane hoto, kowane daƙiƙa, kowane harafi, ko kowace search-unit) idan bayanin farashi yana samuwa; in ba haka ba, `0` ne (fail-open).

> **Ma’anar kuɗi idan cache-hit ya faru:** idan semantic-cache ya yi HIT (`X-OmniRoute-Cache-Hit: true`) ba a yin upstream call, don haka `X-OmniRoute-Response-Cost` zai kasance `0.0000000000` (kuɗin **ƙari** na bayar da hit ɗin). Ana bayar da rahoton ainihin kuɗin/kuɗin da da an kashe dabam a cikin `X-OmniRoute-Cost-Saved`. Masu amfani da bayanan billing su tara jimillar `X-OmniRoute-Response-Cost` (hits ba su da kuɗi); nazarin cache kuma zai iya tara `X-OmniRoute-Cost-Saved`.

## Keɓantattun Hayoyin Zama da Ake Sarrafawa

Hayar zama ta keɓantaccen sarrafawa yarjejeniyar turawa ce ta zaɓi, wadda ba ta karkata ga wani abokin ciniki: mai aiki ɗaya
ne ke riƙe da haɗin OmniRoute ɗaya da ya cancanta. Ba ta hayar da wani samfuri, ba ta buƙatar OAuth, ba ta gano
wani takamaiman abokin ciniki, kuma ba ta buƙatar wani takamaiman mai samarwa.

Dole ne maɓallin API da ake tantancewa da shi ya kasance da izinin `lease:exclusive` da kuma bayyanannen jerin
`allowedConnections` wanda ba fanko ba. Iyakar sauyin bayanan rumbun bayanai tana tilasta amfani da duka filayen
tare yayin ƙirƙirar maɓalli da sabuntawa na ɓangare.

```http
POST /api/v1/session-leases
Authorization: Bearer <managed-api-key>
Content-Type: application/json
X-OmniRoute-Lease-Owner: vlo_<43-base64url-characters>

{"action":"acquire","model":"glm/glm-4.6"}
```

Amsoshin nasarar samu, sabuntawa, da saki suna bayyana tambarin lokaci, `state`, da ainihin ƙimar tabbatacciyar
`generation`, amma ba sa taɓa bayyana haɗin da aka zaɓa ko bayanan shiga. Sabuntawa da saki suna aika
generation a jikin JSON:

```json
{ "action": "renew", "generation": 1 }
```

```json
{ "action": "release", "generation": 1, "reason": "OWNER_EXIT" }
```

Mai hayar da ke aiki zai iya neman bayanan nuni masu kiyaye sirri kai tsaye domin ɗaurinsa na yanzu:

```json
{ "action": "status", "generation": 1 }
```

```json
{
  "state": "ACTIVE",
  "generation": 1,
  "acquiredAt": "2026-08-28T12:00:00.000Z",
  "renewedAt": "2026-08-28T12:00:30.000Z",
  "expiresAt": "2026-08-28T12:02:30.000Z",
  "connection": {
    "displayName": "Primary Codex",
    "provider": "codex"
  }
}
```

Wannan aikin matsayin na zaɓi ana katange shi ta mai shi marar bayyanar ainihi, ingantaccen maɓallin API da ake sarrafawa, da ainihin
generation mai aiki a cikin mu’amalar rumbun bayanai guda ɗaya. `displayName` sunan haɗin da aka saita ne kawai
bayan cire sararin da ya wuce kima; yana zama `null` idan babu amintaccen suna da aka saita. OmniRoute ba ya taɓa maye gurbinsa da
imel ko ƙirƙirarren bayanin asusun mai amfani. Ƙimar mai samarwa lakabin nuni ne mara muhimmancin sirri, kuma ba ta taɓa zama
ƙirƙirarren mai gano mai samarwa mai jituwa ba. An cire bayanan shiga, token, cookies, ainihin lambobin haɗi ko na maɓallin
API, hash na mai shi, sirrin katange shiga, da bayanan turawa na ciki.

Binciken da ke amfani da maɓalli mara daidai, mai shi mara daidai, generation da ya tsufa, ko wanda babu shi, ya ƙare, aka saki, ko aka soke, duk suna
mayar da kuskuren `409 LEASE_FENCE_STALE` iri ɗaya ba tare da bayanan haɗi ba. Abokin ciniki da ya karɓi amsar jiran ƙarfin aiki ba shi da ɗauri mai aiki da zai duba. Lokacin da turawa ta sauya hayar da ke aiki,
generation ɗin nan ɗin yana ci gaba da aiki, kuma matsayin yana mayar da sabon ɗaurin ta hanyar atomik, ba tsohon ba.
Abokan ciniki da ake da su ba sa canzawa saboda amsoshin samu, sabuntawa, saki, da jira suna riƙe
da tsarinsu na baya.

Wannan yarjejeniyar sabar ba ta canza daidaitaccen `/status` na OpenAI Codex. A halin yanzu, daidaitaccen Codex yana bayar da rahoton
mai samar da samfurinsa da ginannen yanayin tantancewa/asusu, amma ba ya nuna bayanan asusun wani mai samarwa na musamman
ba; haɗawar abokin ciniki ta gaba dole ne ta kira wannan aikin sannan ta yanke shawarar yadda za ta
nuna `connection.displayName`.

Sa’an nan kowace buƙatar inference da ake sarrafawa tana aika dukkan shugabannin sarrafawa biyu:

```http
X-OmniRoute-Lease-Owner: vlo_<43-base64url-characters>
X-OmniRoute-Lease-Generation: 1
```

Ana katange ainihin mai shi, generation, haɗin da ke aiki, da ingantaccen maɓallin API nan take
kafin kowane yunƙurin upstream da ake tallafawa. Sake amfani da mai shi da generation tare da wani maɓalli zai gaza ko da
maɓallin yana ba da izinin haɗi iri ɗaya. Ba a adana ainihin masu shi, rubuta su a rajista, riƙe su a cikin
hoton buƙatar, ko tura su zuwa upstream.

Cunkoso na ɗan lokaci yana mayar da HTTP `429` tare da `Retry-After` da:

```json
{
  "state": "WAITING_FOR_CAPACITY",
  "error": { "type": "lease_error", "code": "LEASE_CAPACITY_UNAVAILABLE" },
  "reason": "NO_FREE_ELIGIBLE_CONNECTION",
  "retryAfter": 30
}
```

Wannan amsar tana nufin kawai cewa jerin waɗanda suka cancanta na yau da kullum ba fanko ba ne, kuma kowane ɗan takara mai ’yanci
yana ƙarƙashin hayar wani daban da ke aiki. Samfura/masu samarwa marasa tallafi, rashin dacewa da manufofi, lokacin jiran sake amfani, ƙayyadadden amfani,
lafiya, da sauran gazawar cancanta ta yau da kullum suna ci gaba da amfani da amsoshin OmniRoute da ake da su.

### `x-omniroute-compression`

Sauya tsarin matsawa ga kowace buƙata. Shi ne mafi fifiko — ya fi sauyin haɗin turawa,
profile mai aiki, kunnawa ta atomatik, da Default na panel. Ƙimomi:

| Ƙima          | Tasiri                                                                                                                     |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `off`         | Babu matsawa ga wannan buƙatar.                                                                                            |
| `default`     | Default profile da aka samo daga panel (yana yin watsi da profile mai aiki). Ana barin injunan da ke rasa bayanai a kashe. |
| `safe`        | Cire maimaituwa da haɗa sararin rubutu kawai.                                                                              |
| `allow-lossy` | Riƙe tsarin mai aiki ga wannan buƙatar, ciki har da taƙaitawa da sake rubuta salo.                                         |
| `engine:<id>` | Inji guda ɗaya idan an kunna shi, misali `engine:rtk`. Zaɓin amfani da wannan injin ga kowace buƙata.                      |
| `<combo>`     | Combo mai suna, wanda ake fara daidaita shi ta suna (ba tare da la’akari da manya ko ƙananan haruffa ba), sannan ta id.    |

Bayanan kula:

- Ana yin watsi da ƙimomin da ba a sani ba (ba a taɓa ƙin buƙatar); warwarewar tana komawa ga fifikon mai aiki na yau da kullum.
- Idan combo da yawa suna da suna iri ɗaya, aika **id** na combo domin samun daidaitaccen zaɓi.
- Ba za a iya zaɓar combo mai suna `off` ko `default` ta suna ba (ana fara fassara waɗannan kalmomin musamman); ambaci irin wannan combo ta id ɗinsa.
- Babban maɓallin matsawa ƙaƙƙarfan shamaki ne: idan an kashe matsawa gaba ɗaya, wannan header ba zai iya kunna shi ba.

Ana maimaita tsarin da aka yi amfani da shi a cikin header na amsa:

```
X-OmniRoute-Compression: <mode>; source=<source>
```

inda `<source>` yake ɗaya daga cikin `request-header`, `routing-override`, `active-profile`, `auto-trigger`, `default`, ko `off`.

## Embeddings

```bash
POST /v1/embeddings
Authorization: Bearer your-api-key
Content-Type: application/json

{
  "model": "nebius/Qwen/Qwen3-Embedding-8B",
  "input": "The food was delicious"
}
```

Masu samarwa da ake da su: Nebius, OpenAI, Mistral, Together AI, Fireworks, NVIDIA, **OpenRouter**, Jina AI.

ID na katalog su ne `provider/model` (misali: `jina-ai/jina-embeddings-v5-omni-small`). ID na samfurin Jina marasa sunan mai samarwa waɗanda suke cikin rajista (misali `jina-embeddings-v5-text-small`, `jina-reranker-v3.5`) su ma suna aiki. Ayyukan embed/rerank/classify/segment na Jina suna fara amfani da bayanan shaidar `jina-ai` na dashboard; `JINA_AI_API_KEY` madadin ne kawai idan babu maɓallin dashboard. Katin `jina-reader` na Reader / `r.jina.ai` ne kawai (`POST /v1/web/fetch`), kuma ba ya taɓa samar da embeddings ko rerank.

Samfuran rajista waɗanda suka bayyana goyon bayan multimodal suna kuma karɓar har zuwa abubuwa tsararru 32 masu zaman kansu daga mai samarwa.
Nau'ikan abubuwan kafofin watsa labarai su ne `text`, `image`, `audio`, `video`, da `document`. `source` na kafar watsa labaransu
ko dai `{"type":"url","url":"https://..."}` ne, ko kuma
`{"type":"base64","data":"...","media_type":"..."}`.

Jina v5 Omni (`jina-ai/jina-embeddings-v5-omni-small`, `jina-ai/jina-embeddings-v5-omni-nano`,
da laƙabin iyali `jina-ai/jina-embeddings-v5-omni` → omni-small) yana kuma karɓar takardun EmbeddingsV5Request na asali na Jina,
kuma yana **tura su yadda suke ba tare da canji ba** zuwa `https://api.jina.ai/v1/embeddings`:

```json
{
  "model": "jina-ai/jina-embeddings-v5-omni-small",
  "task": "retrieval.query",
  "normalized": true,
  "input": [
    { "text": "a red bicycle" },
    { "image": "https://example.com/bike.png" },
    { "content": [{ "text": "caption" }, { "image": "data:image/png;base64,..." }] }
  ]
}
```

Ƙimomin asali na `{ image | audio | video | pdf }` na iya zama URL na HTTPS na jama'a, URI na `data:`, ko ɗanyen
base64. OmniRoute ba ya sauya waɗannan abubuwan zuwa string ko ɗauko URL na hotuna na asali — Jina ne ke ɗauko
kafofin watsa labarai na jama'a da kansa. Ana tura ƙarin filayen Jina (`task`, `normalized`, `truncate`, `embedding_type`).
SKU na Jina masu rubutu kaɗai har yanzu suna ƙin takardun da ba rubutu ba.

Iyakokin tsaro da jigilar bayanai:

- Dole ne URL na kafofin watsa labarai na nesa su kasance HTTPS na jama'a. Ana ɗauko abubuwan canonical na `{type,source:url}`
  a gefen uwar garke (sake tabbatar da karkatarwa, wa'adin lokaci, iyakokin girma, DNS na jama'a, da kulle haɗi), sannan a
  saka su kai tsaye kafin kiran mai samarwa. Ana tura abubuwan asali na Jina na `{image:"https://..."}` yadda suke
  bayan an yi binciken HTTPS na jama'a iri ɗaya; Jina ne ke ɗauko URL ɗin.
- An iyakance kafofin watsa labarai na base64 da aka saka kai tsaye zuwa 8 MiB bayan decoding ga kowane abu, da 16 MiB bayan decoding a duk buƙatar.

Fassarar mai samarwa (ba a taɓa tura abubuwan canonical ba tare da canji ba):

- Samfuran Jina na multimodal: kowane abu na matakin sama yana zama abu guda mai maɓallin modality
  (`text` / `image` / `audio` / `video` / `pdf`) ta amfani da URI na data don kafofin watsa labarai da aka saka kai tsaye; vector ɗaya ga
  kowane abu na matakin sama.
- Iyalin Gemini Embedding 2: array guda na matakin sama yana zama buƙatar asali guda ta
  `models/{model}:embedContent` mai `content.parts` (`text` ko `inline_data`).
- llama.cpp (`llama-cpp/<model>`, duk samfurin da uwar garken gida ta loda): abubuwan canonical na `text`
  suna zama strings na yau da kullum, yayin da `image` / `audio` / `video` suke zama abu guda na
  `{"content": [part]}` kowannensu, ta amfani da sassan abun cikin chat na llama-server (`image_url`,
  `input_audio` mai tsarin `wav` / `mp3` / `flac`, `input_video`) tare da bayanan da aka saka kai tsaye; vector ɗaya
  ga kowane abu na matakin sama. Dole ne uwar garken ta yi aiki da `--embedding --mmproj …`; idan babu projector,
  ita da kanta za ta ƙi kafofin watsa labarai. Ba a goyon bayan `document`.
- Samfuran da ba a sani ba/masu canzawa waɗanda ba su da metadata na modality a bayyane suna ƙin shigarwar da aka tsara da HTTP 400.

```json
{
  "model": "jina-ai/jina-embeddings-v5-omni-small",
  "input": [
    { "type": "text", "text": "A red bicycle" },
    {
      "type": "image",
      "source": { "type": "url", "url": "https://example.com/bicycle.png" }
    }
  ],
  "dimensions": 512,
  "encoding_format": "float"
}
```

Haɗin samfur/modality da ba a goyon baya yana mayar da HTTP 400 maimakon tilasta sauya abin. Filayen tsawo waɗanda ba na input ba
a kan tsofaffin buƙatun string/token suna ci gaba da wucewa ba tare da canji ba.

```bash
# Jera dukkan samfuran embedding
GET /v1/embeddings
```

API na `analyze` na Azure Document Intelligence ba ya aiki tare: buƙatar farko tana dawo da

daidaita amsar ƙarshe ta Azure zuwa tsarin `pages`/`markdown` iri ɗaya da Mistral ke amfani da shi kafin a dawo da ita ga

`providerSpecificData.project`/`providerSpecificData.region` da aka ayyana kai tsaye koyaushe su ne za su yi aiki;

yana komawa ga tsohon zaɓin `us-central1`. Dukkan warwarewar biyu suna faruwa a `open-sse/handlers/ocr.ts`
(`resolveVertexOcrAccessToken`, `resolveVertexOcrBaseUrl`), sannan
`src/app/api/v1/ocr/route.ts` yana amfani da su kafin turawa zuwa `handleOcr`.

## Jerin Samfura

```bash
Authorization: Bearer your-api-key
```

### Gaban id na samfur (`?prefix=`)

Yawancin samfura ana tallata su ƙarƙashin **gaban mai samarwa**. Gaban da za ka samu yana ƙarƙashin ikon
tutar fasalin `MODELS_CATALOG_PREFIX_MODE`, kuma ana iya sauya shi **ga kowace buƙata** ta amfani da
sigar tambaya — wannan yana da amfani ga abokin hulɗa da ke son jeri mai tsabta ba tare da sauya saitin
uwar garken gaba ɗaya ga kowa ba:

```bash
GET /v1/models?prefix=alias        # id guda ga kowane samfur — gajeren gaban alias
GET /v1/models?prefix=dual         # duka nau'ikan biyu (tsohon saitin uwar garke)
GET /v1/models?prefix=canonical    # cikakken gaban provider-id kawai
```

| Yanayi      | Abin da yake fitarwa                                     | Bayanan kula                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `dual`      | `cc/claude-sonnet-4-6` **da** `claude/claude-sonnet-4-6` | **Tsohon saiti.** Duka id biyun suna turawa zuwa samfur ɗaya; an riƙe su domin saitunan abokan hulɗa da suka ƙayyade ɗaya daga cikin nau'ikan su ci gaba da aiki. Yana kusan ninka kundin sau biyu.                    |
| `alias`     | `cc/claude-sonnet-4-6`                                   | Shigarwa guda ga kowane samfur. Masu samarwa da ba su da alias na daban har yanzu suna fitar da shigarwarsu, don haka babu abin da ya ɓace.                                                                            |
| `canonical` | `claude/claude-sonnet-4-6`                               | Shigarwa guda ga kowane samfur ƙarƙashin cikakken gaban provider-id. Masu samarwa da ba su da alias na daban (misali `antigravity/…`, `agy/…`) su ma suna fitar da id ɗinsu guda a nan, don haka babu abin da ya ɓace. |

Hakanan ana iya gane madubin yanayin `dual` ba tare da sigar tambayar ba: yana ɗauke da filin `parent`
wanda ke nuna babban id.

Ya kamata abokan hulɗa da ke nuna mai zaɓen samfur su nemi `?prefix=alias` — wannan shi ne abin da
[ƙarin OmniCopilot na VS Code](../guides/VSCODE-COPILOT.md) yake yi.

### Matatun ganowa (`?capability=`, `?min_context=`, `?kind=`, `?q=`)

`GET /v1/models` yana karɓar matatun ganowa na zaɓi. Idan babu ɗayansu, amsar ba ta
canza. Suna taƙaita kundin da mai kira ya riga ya gani (ana fara amfani da izinin API-key),
kuma ana aiwatar da su kafin tsarin rabon shafuka na OpenAI mai amfani da `limit` / `after`:

```bash
GET /v1/models?capability=vision&capability=tool_calling   # dole ne kowace damar da aka jera ta zama gaskiya
GET /v1/models?capability=vision,reasoning&min_context=200000
GET /v1/models?kind=embedding                              # `type` na kundin; layukan da ba su da shi su ne `chat`
GET /v1/models?q=sonnet&limit=10                           # daidaitawa ba tare da kula da manyan ko ƙananan haruffa ba a kan id ko suna
```

| Siga          | Ƙimomi                                                                                              |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `capability`  | `attachment`, `reasoning`, `structured_output`, `temperature`, `thinking`, `tool_calling`, `vision` |
| `min_context` | cikakkiyar lamba marar korau, ana kwatanta ta da `context_length`                                   |
| `kind`        | `chat`, `audio`, `embedding`, `image`, `moderation`, `music`, `rerank`, `video`                     |
| `q`           | haruffa 1–200                                                                                       |

Ƙimar da ba a sani ba, ko `min_context` wanda ba cikakkiyar lamba marar korau ba ne (misali `1.5`),
yana dawo da HTTP `400`, kuma saƙon yana jera ingantattun ƙimomi. Aiwatarwa:
`src/app/api/v1/models/catalogFilters.ts`.

### Taƙaitaccen jerin samfura (`POST /v1/models/query`)

`POST /v1/models/query` yana dawo da gajeren jeri mai daidaitaccen sakamako daga kundin ɗaya, tare da
irin wannan tantancewa da ganin da aka keɓe wa kowace maɓalli kamar `GET /v1/models`:

```bash
POST /v1/models/query
Authorization: Bearer your-api-key
Content-Type: application/json

{ "capabilities": ["vision", "tool_calling"], "min_context": 128000, "max_output": 16000,
  "kind": "chat", "q": "gpt", "owned_by": "openai", "exclude": ["openai/gpt-4o"], "limit": 5 }

→ { "object": "list", "data": [ ...samfura ] }
```

Kowane fili na zaɓi ne. `capabilities` da `exclude` suna karɓar jeri ko kirtani da aka raba da wakafi;
`max_output` yana kwatantawa da `max_output_tokens`. Ana jera sakamako bisa `owned_by`, sannan `id`.
Tsohon saitin `limit` shi ne `20`, kuma ana iyakance ƙimomin da suka wuce `100` zuwa `100`. JSON mara inganci, fili
da ba a sani ba, ko ƙima marar inganci yana dawo da HTTP `400`.

### Samfuran hira da aka ɓoye ɗaya-ɗaya

Samfurin da aka yi wa alamar **Ɓoye** a shafin mai samar da shi ba ya cikin kundin, kuma ana ƙi
shi da HTTP `404` / `model_not_found` idan aka nema kai tsaye. Binciken yana amfani da
mai samarwa da samfurin da aka warware, ciki har da alias na masu samarwa, gaban kumburin mai samarwa
mai jituwa, da tsoffin saitunan haɗi. Haɗin samfura yana tsallake manufofin da aka ɓoye kuma yana iya amfani da
takwaran da ake iya gani; idan babu wata manufa mai iya aiki da ta rage, yana dawo da lambar kuskure ɗaya. Cire ɓoyewar
yana fara aiki a buƙata ta gaba. Sauye-sauyen ganin hoto kaɗai ba sa ɓoye samfurin hira mai ID iri ɗaya.

Wannan saitin samfur ɗaya-ɗaya ya bambanta da
[jeren yarda/hanawa na bayyana samfur](../routing/MODEL_EXPOSURE_LIST.md), wanda ke tace tallan kundin
da 'yan takarar turawa ta atomatik yayin da yake riƙe aikawa kai tsaye. Izinin samfur na API-key
yana ci gaba da aiki dabam. Tsohon yanayin gaban kundin ya kasance `dual`.

### Nau'ikan samfur marasa tunani

Ga samfuran Claude masu iya tunani, `/v1/models` yana kuma tallata nau'in **marar tunani** wanda aka sanya wa id ɗinsa gaban `claude-3-omniroute-no-thinking/`:

```
claude-3-omniroute-no-thinking/<provider>/<model>
```

Zaɓin wannan id (misali, a cikin saitin Claude Code wanda koyaushe yake haɗa tubalin `thinking`) yana komawa ga ainihin `<provider>/<model>` tare da kashe yin tunani — `thinking:{type:"disabled"}` a hanyar `/v1/messages`, ko kuma a cire filayen `reasoning`/`reasoning_effort` a hanyar `/v1/chat/completions`. Ana lissafa wannan nau'in ne kawai ga samfuran dangin Claude waɗanda ke goyon bayan yin tunani **kuma** suke mutunta `disabled` (saboda haka, misali, an cire samfuran da ke aiki da yanayin daidaitawa kawai waɗanda ke ƙin `disabled`). Masu gudanarwa za su iya tilasta kunna ko kashe wannan nau'in ga kowane samfuri ta hanyar `ModelSpec.noThinkingAlias`.

## Samar da sauti na ElevenLabs

Saita haɗin mai bayarwa na `elevenlabs` a cikin OmniRoute tare da maɓallin API ɗinka na ElevenLabs.
Abokan ciniki suna tabbatar da kansu da maɓallin **OmniRoute** nasu (`Authorization: Bearer $OMNIROUTE_API_KEY`);
gateway yana bayar da maɓallin mai bayarwa da aka adana a matsayin `xi-api-key`. Kada ka taɓa aika maɓallin ElevenLabs
a matsayin bearer token na gateway.

| Hanya | Path                        | Martani                                       | Rukunin endpoint na maɓallin API |
| ----- | --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| POST  | `/v1/sound-generation`      | Bytes na sauti na asali                       | `elevenlabs`                     |
| POST  | `/v1/music`                 | Bytes na sauti na asali                       | `music`                          |
| POST  | `/v1/music/stream`          | Bytes na sauti masu gudana                    | `music`                          |
| POST  | `/v1/music/detailed`        | Sautin multipart na asali da tsarin tsarawa   | `music`                          |
| POST  | `/v1/music/detailed/stream` | Metadata na SSE na asali da gutsutsuren sauti | `music`                          |
| POST  | `/v1/music/plan`            | Tsarin tsarawa na JSON                        | `music`                          |

Waɗannan endpoints suna ƙara wa hanyoyin `/v1/audio/speech`, `/v1/audio/transcriptions`,
`/v1/voices`, `/v1/text-to-speech/{voiceId}` da `/v1/speech-to-text` da ake da su. Ana kiyaye filayen JSON na asali
da query parameters kamar `output_format` da `enable_logging`.
Martanin `/v1/music/detailed` yana riƙe iyakokin multipart ɗinsa; a fassara shi a matsayin multipart.
`/v1/music/detailed/stream` yana dawo da `text/event-stream` tare da metadata na tsarawa da
gutsutsuren sauti na base64 a cikin abubuwan SSE. Duk hanyoyi shida suna goyon bayan CORS preflight.

```bash
export OMNIROUTE_BASE_URL="http://localhost:20128"
export OMNIROUTE_API_KEY="your-omniroute-api-key"

# Gajeren tasirin sauti. Tsawon lokaci: daƙiƙa 0.5 zuwa 30.
curl --fail-with-body "$OMNIROUTE_BASE_URL/v1/sound-generation?output_format=mp3_44100_128" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OMNIROUTE_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"text":"A single soft bell chime","duration_seconds":1,"model_id":"eleven_text_to_sound_v2"}' \
  --output effect.mp3

# Ana bayyana tsawon kiɗa a millisekondi: 3000 zuwa 600000.
curl --fail-with-body "$OMNIROUTE_BASE_URL/v1/music?output_format=mp3_44100_128" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OMNIROUTE_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"prompt":"Gentle instrumental piano","music_length_ms":10000,"force_instrumental":true,"model_id":"music_v1"}' \
  --output music.mp3
```

Yi amfani da `/v1/music/stream` tare da JSON body iri ɗaya don isar da sauti a hankali-a-hankali. Tsarawa
yana karɓar ɗaya tak kacal daga cikin `prompt` ko `composition_plan`; `/v1/music/plan` yana buƙatar `prompt`
kuma zai iya karɓar `source_composition_plan` idan ana so. Schemas na asali suna kiyaye ƙarin
filayen mai bayarwa domin sigogin tsarin tsarawa da zaɓuɓɓukan daidaitawa su isa ElevenLabs.

Haɗaɗɗen endpoint na `/v1/music/generations` shi ma yana karɓar `elevenlabs/music_v1`,
`elevenlabs/music_v2` da `elevenlabs/music_v2_5`. Idan akwai haɗin ElevenLabs mai aiki,
ana nuna waɗannan models a cikin kundin kiɗa. Wannan endpoint yana dawo da
`{"created":...,"data":[{"b64_json":"...","format":"mp3"}]}`. Filin `duration` nasa yana cikin
daƙiƙoƙi kuma yana daidaituwa da `music_length_ms`; `music_length_ms` da aka bayyana kai tsaye shi ne yake da fifiko.
`instrumental` yana daidaituwa da `force_instrumental`, inda filin asali yake da fifiko.
Yi amfani da endpoints na streaming na asali don streaming, da endpoint na tsarawa na asali
don buƙatun da tsarin tsarawa kaɗai ke jagoranta.

```bash
curl --fail-with-body "$OMNIROUTE_BASE_URL/v1/music/generations" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OMNIROUTE_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"model":"elevenlabs/music_v1","prompt":"Gentle instrumental piano","duration":10,"instrumental":true}'
```

Ƙuntatawar endpoint, model, kasafin kuɗi da haɗi suna aiki kafin kiran upstream. Hanyoyin
asali suna dawo da lambobin matsayi da jikin kurakurai na upstream ba tare da canji ba, ciki har da `402` don
ƙuntatawar tsari da `429` don iyakar quota. Ana kiyaye headers na `character-cost`, `song-id`, `request-id` da `retry-after`
idan suna nan. Samun ragowar credit kyauta ba ya tabbatar da samun damar kowane
model na kiɗa, tsarin fitarwa ko fasalin API: ElevenLabs ne ke yanke shawarar haƙƙin kowane buƙata.
Haɗaɗɗen endpoint yana kiyaye lambobin matsayin kuskure a cikin daidaitaccen error envelope na OmniRoute.

Yarjejeniyoyin upstream: [tasirin sauti](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/text-to-sound-effects/convert),
[tsara kiɗa](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/music/compose) da
[cikakkun bayanan kiɗa mai gudana](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/music/compose-detailed-stream).

> **Kumburin sake-jera na nodes na mai bayarwa:** `POST /v1/rerank` yana kuma turawa zuwa nodes na masu bayarwa masu dacewa da OpenAI
> (oMLX, vLLM, Infinity, TEI a bayan gateway, …) waɗanda ake yi wa adireshi a matsayin `<node-prefix>/<model>`. Nodes na loopback
> (`localhost`, `127.0.0.1`, `172.16.0.0/12`) koyaushe sun cancanta, haka kuma sunayen hosts da
> mai gudanarwa ya jera a cikin `OMNIROUTE_LOCAL_PROVIDER_NODE_HOSTS` (misali sunan sabis na Docker/Compose kamar
> `http://reranker:8080/v1`; ana kiransu kai tsaye, ba ta hanyar `HTTP(S)_PROXY` ko proxy da aka ɗaure wa
> haɗi ba). Nodes da ke kan kowane host dabam
> — na'ura a LAN ko takwaran Tailscale — suna cancanta ne kawai idan mai gudanarwa ya kunna
> tutar fasalin `RERANK_REMOTE_PROVIDER_NODES` **kuma** base URL na node ɗin ya wuce manufar URL
> mai fita ta mai bayarwa (`OMNIROUTE_ALLOW_LOCAL_PROVIDER_URLS` / `OMNIROUTE_ALLOW_PRIVATE_PROVIDER_URLS`).
> Matakin sake-jera na injin memory yana kiran wannan route ta hanyar
> loopback, don haka ƙa'ida ɗaya ce ke sarrafa `rerankProviderModel` a cikin saitunan Memory.
>
> **Tsarukan uwar garken gida:** ana kiran node ɗin a `<base>/v1/rerank`, kuma idan an samu 404, a `<base>/rerank`
> (Infinity, TEI). Body da ake turawa zuwa upstream yana ɗauke da rubutun Cohere/OpenAI (`documents`,
> `return_documents`) da kuma rubutun TEI (`texts`, `return_text`), sannan ana daidaita martanin upstream
> zuwa envelope na Cohere: jerin TEI kai tsaye na `[{index, score, text}]`, `{results: [{index, score}]}`
> daga ƙananan gateways, da kuma na salon Voyage `{data: [...]}` duk suna komawa ga abokin ciniki a matsayin
> `{results: [{index, relevance_score, document?}]}`, an jera su bisa score kuma an iyakance su zuwa `top_n`.

> **Gano nodes na mai bayarwa:** models da ke kan node na mai bayarwa mai dacewa da OpenAI suna bayyana a `GET /v1/models`
> ƙarƙashin prefix na node ɗin. Layukan da ba su ɗauke da metadata na endpoint ba (abin da aka saba gani a jerin `/v1/models` na gida)
> suna gaji `apiType` na node ɗin, don haka models na node na `embeddings` suna zama `type: "embedding"` kuma models na
> node na `rerank` suna zama `type: "rerank"` maimakon komawa zuwa chat a matsayin tsoho; bayyanannen
> `supportedEndpoints` a kan layin da aka daidaita ko aka ƙara da hannu har yanzu shi ne ke da fifiko.

### `provider_options` na Exa da Tavily

Exa da Tavily suna karanta filayen da suka keɓanta ga mai samarwa daga jakar da aka ware da namespace:
`provider_options.exa` (ko `provider_options["exa-search"]`) da `provider_options.tavily`
(ko `provider_options["tavily-search"]`). Maɓallan da ke ƙasa kawai ake turawa. Ana
duba kowannensu da nasa Zod schema a cikin `open-sse/handlers/search/providerOptionsPassthrough.ts`,
kuma ana cire maɓalli mai nau'in da bai dace ba. Ana yin watsi da duk wani maɓalli, ciki har da auth, base URL,
`stream` da `outputSchema`. Maɓallan flat da sauran masu samarwa ke amfani da su (`cx`, `depth`, ...) suna aiki
kamar yadda suke yi a baya.

| Mai samarwa | Filayen matakin sama                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Masu ciki                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Exa         | `type`, `category`, `userLocation`, `moderation`, `additionalQueries`, `objective`, `systemPrompt`, `compliance` (flat primitives), `startPublishedDate`, `endPublishedDate`                                                                                                                                                                                              | Ana haɗa `contents.{text, highlights, summary, extras, subpages, subpageTarget, livecrawlTimeout, maxAgeHours}` a kan tsohon ƙimar `{ text: true, highlights: true }` |
| Tavily      | `search_depth`, `chunks_per_source`, `time_range`, `start_date`, `end_date`, `include_published_date`, `filter_by_published_date`, `include_answer`, `include_raw_content`, `include_images`, `include_image_descriptions`, `include_favicon`, `include_domains_mode`, `language`, `filter_by_language`, `auto_parameters`, `exact_match`, `include_usage`, `safe_search` | —                                                                                                                                                                     |

Filayen gama-gari koyaushe suna da fifiko: `query`, `max_results`, `filters` (domains), `country` da kuma
topic da aka samo daga `search_type`. A Exa, `search_type: "news"` yana riƙe da `category: "news"`.
Tavily kuma yana karɓar `search_type: "finance"` (`topic: "finance"`).

Yanzu `time_range` na gama-gari yana isa ga duka masu samarwa: Exa yana samun `startPublishedDate` (ISO
timestamp), Tavily kuma yana samun `start_date` (`YYYY-MM-DD`). Kwanan wata da aka fayyace kai tsaye a cikin `provider_options`
yana maye gurbin wanda aka tsara.

Sakamakon Exa yana riƙe da `id`, `highlight_scores`, `summary`, `subpages`, `entities` da `extras` a cikin
`provider_raw`. Sakamakon Tavily yana riƙe da `images` da `favicon`.

Lissafin kuɗi har yanzu yana amfani da flat registry `costPerQuery`. Tavily yana cajin
`search_depth: "advanced"` da ƙarin credits a kowane kira, kuma wannan bambancin ba ya bayyana a cikin
`usage.search_cost_usd`.

## API na Ɗauko Bayanai daga Yanar Gizo

Ciro abun ciki daga URL ta hanyar mai samar da aikin ɗauko bayanai daga yanar gizo da aka saita (Firecrawl, Jina
Reader, Tavily Extract, TinyFish Fetch, Nimble Extract, Parallel Extract).

Parallel Extract (`parallel-search`, maɓallin API iri ɗaya da na Parallel Search) yana mayar da Markdown
kawai — duk wani `format` dabam yana haifar da `400` — kuma yana iyakance abun ciki zuwa haruffa 100,000. Idan
ba za a iya ciro shafin da ake nufi da kansa ba, sabis na sama yana bayar da rahoton hakan a cikin HTTP 200;
OmniRoute yana gabatar da shi a matsayin `422` tare da tsayayyen saƙo.

| Hanya | Tafarki         | Bayani                                                     |
| ----- | --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| POST  | `/v1/web/fetch` | Ɗauko/goge URL — `v1WebFetchSchema` yana tantance body ɗin |

**Tantancewa:** Maɓallin API na Bearer (`extractApiKey` + `isValidApiKey`). Ana aiwatar da manufofin ta hanyar `enforceApiKeyPolicy`.

**Sauya-mai-samarwa mai la'akari da ƙayyadadden amfani (#8297):** idan ba a bayar da takamaiman `provider` ba, za a bi jerin
(`firecrawl` → `jina-reader` → `tavily-search` → `tinyfish` → `nimble-search` →
`parallel-search`) bisa tsayayyen
tsarin fifiko (cika-na-farko) — za a tsallake mai samarwa da aka saita amma aka taƙaita yawan buƙatunsa
maimakon dakatar da buƙatar nan take, sannan gazawar sabis na sama da za a iya sake gwadawa/ta ƙayyadadden amfani
(HTTP 429 a koyaushe; 402/403 ga matakan kyauta masu salon ƙayyadadden amfani na Firecrawl/Tavily/TinyFish —
ba ga Jina Reader ba, kuma ba a taɓa yin haka ga buƙata mara kyau ta 400 kawai) za ta wuce zuwa
mai samarwa na gaba da ba a gwada ba, wanda ke da bayanan izini, a lokacin buƙatar. Idan duk masu samarwa a cikin
jerin sun ƙare, endpoint ɗin zai mayar da `429` guda ɗaya (tare da header na `Retry-After`)
maimakon tsohon `400` na gama-gari. Idan an buƙaci takamaiman `provider`,
**ba a yin** sauyawa a ɓoye — takamaiman mai samarwa da aka taƙaita yawan buƙatunsa ko ya gaza
zai nuna kuskurensa (`429` idan an taƙaita yawan buƙatunsa, in ba haka ba matsayin
sabis na sama).

## Yaɗawa ta WebSocket

```bash
GET /v1/ws?handshake=1
```

Yana tantance musayar buɗe haɗin WebSocket kuma yana mayar da misalan saƙonnin ka'idar sadarwa (`request`, `cancel`). Sabar WS da aka haɗa da tsarin ce ke sarrafa ainihin firamomin WS a wajen teburin hanyoyin Next.js.

**Tantancewa:** Maɓallin API na Bearer yayin musayar buɗe haɗi.

### Responses API ta WebSocket (codex kawai)

```bash
# Host:port iri ɗaya da na HTTP API (tsoho 20128); ɗaukaka haɗin:
wscat -c "ws://localhost:20128/v1/responses?api_key=<OMNIROUTE_API_KEY>"
# (ko: -H "Authorization: Bearer <OMNIROUTE_API_KEY>")

# Dole ne firam na farko ya zama response.create:
{ "type": "response.create", "model": "gpt-5.5", "input": [ { "role": "user", "content": "hi" } ] }
```

An haɗa proxy na Responses-API-ta-WebSocket **da `codex` kaɗai** (backend na ChatGPT).
Yana sauraro a port ɗaya da API/dashboard a hanyoyin `/v1/responses`,
`/responses`, da `/api/v1/responses`. A firam na farko na `response.create`, yana
tantancewa + shiryawa ta gadar cikin gida ta `codex-responses-ws`, yana zaɓar
haɗin OAuth na codex, sannan yana kafa tunnel zuwa `wss://chatgpt.com/backend-api/codex/responses`
ta hanyar jigilar `wreq-js`. **Ana ƙin samfuran da ba codex ba** (`codex_ws_provider_required`).
Don routing na raba ƙayyadadden amfani, yi amfani da `model: "qtSd/<group>/codex/<model>"`. An aiwatar da shi a cikin
`app/server-ws.mjs` + `scripts/dev/responses-ws-proxy.mjs` + `src/app/api/internal/codex-responses-ws/route.ts`.

**Tantancewa:** Maɓallin API na Bearer yayin musayar buɗe haɗi. Dole ne sabar HTTP da aka haɗa (`server-ws.mjs`)
ta kasance entrypoint mai aiki (kuma ita ce ta tsohuwa idan `app/server-ws.mjs` yana nan).

#### ID na samfur: yi amfani da ainihin ID na ChatGPT (ba tare da prefix na `codex/` ba)

OpenAI **Codex CLI** yana tantance sunan samfur a ɓangaren client idan
`supports_websockets = true` kuma yana **ƙin ID masu prefix na mai samarwa** kamar
`codex/gpt-5.5` (`The 'codex/gpt-5.5' model is not supported when using Codex with
a ChatGPT account`). Aika **ainihin** ID (misali `gpt-5.5`). Gadar OmniRoute
ta codex kaɗai ce, saboda haka tana sake warware ainihin ID a matsayin samfurin codex
(`resolveCodexWsModelInfo`) kafin ta kafa tunnel zuwa sabis na sama — duk da cewa ainihin
`gpt-5.5` zai iya zuwa wani mai samarwa ta HTTP in ba haka ba.

#### Saita OpenAI Codex CLI

Nuna Codex CLI zuwa OmniRoute ta ƙara mai samarwa na musamman mai goyon bayan WebSocket
a cikin `~/.codex/config.toml` (yi amfani da `CODEX_HOME` na daban don kauce wa
taɓa saitin da yake akwai):

```toml

model_provider = "omniroute"

[model_providers.omniroute]
name = "OmniRoute (WS)"
```

```bash
export OMNIROUTE_API_KEY=sk-...           # maɓallin API na OmniRoute (kowane maɓalli idan REQUIRE_API_KEY=false)
codex exec "Responda apenas: PONG"
```

CLI yana ɗaukaka `base_url + /responses` zuwa WebSocket, sannan OmniRoute yana kafa masa
tunnel zuwa haɗin OAuth na codex da aka zaɓa. An tantance shi daga farko har ƙarshe da sabar
gida: ChatGPT yana mayar da `codex.rate_limits` + `response.created` kuma yana yaɗa

## Amfani kai tsaye (`/api/usage/om-usage`)

Kowane maɓallin API zai iya karanta amfani da ƙa’idojin amfani **nasa** — ba a buƙatar izinin gudanarwa. Wannan shi ne endpoint da
client (CLI, panel ɗin OmniCopilot) ke amfani da shi don nuna wa mai maɓalli adadin kuɗin da ya kashe.

```bash
# Tsarin rubutu (yarjejeniyar tarihi — rubutu zalla don terminal)
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer <your-api-key>" \
  http://localhost:20128/api/usage/om-usage

# Tsari mai tsararrun bayanai — abin da UI ke amfani da shi
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer <your-api-key>" \
  "http://localhost:20128/api/usage/om-usage?format=json"
```

Dole ne maɓallin ya kunna **`allowUsageCommand`** (a kashe yake ta tsohuwa — manajan maɓallan API na dashboard
yana kunna ko kashe shi ga kowane maɓalli). Idan ba tare da shi ba, endpoint ɗin zai amsa da `403`.

`?format=json` yana mayar da tsari mai bambance yanayi domin mai kira kada ya taɓa karanta filin bayanai daga
amsar ƙin amincewa. Idan an yi nasara:

```jsonc
{
  "allowed": true,
  // yana nan ne kawai idan maɓallin ya zaɓi iyakokin amfani na kowane maɓalli (USD na rana/mako):
  "personal": {
    "dailySpentUsd": 1.25,
    "dailyLimitUsd": 5,
    "dailyResetAtIso": "…",
    "weeklySpentUsd": 8,
    "weeklyLimitUsd": 20,
    "weeklyResetAtIso": "…" /* … */,
  },
  // hoton halin ƙa’idar amfani na provider da aka zaɓa, ko null idan har yanzu babu abin da aka adana:
  "provider": { "connectionId": "…", "provider": "claude", "plan": "…", "quotas": {/* … */} },
  // hoton halin kowace connection, domin UI ya iya nuna providers da dama gefe da gefe:
  "providers": [
    { "connectionId": "…", "provider": "claude" /* … */ },
    { "provider": "codex" /* … */ },
  ],
}
```

Idan an ƙi amincewa (`401` maɓalli mara inganci / `403` ba a yarda ba), wannan route ɗin yana mayar da
`{ "allowed": false, "error": { "message": "…" } }` — kasancewar `personal`/`provider` amma babu komai a ciki
(an yarda da maɓallin, amma har yanzu ba a samo komai ba) yanayi ne daban da ƙin amincewa, kuma tsarin JSON kaɗai
ne ke bambance su.

**Tabbatar da izini:** Maɓallin API na Bearer na mai kiran, wanda aka tantance ta `isValidApiKey` — wannan _ba_
fannin gudanarwa ba ne (`/api/keys/…`), wanda ke ci gaba da kasancewa a bayan `requireManagementAuth`.

### Ɗanyen Kaso da Kason Ƙa’idar Amfani da Aka Daidaita da Iyakar Yankewa

Lokacin amfani da `/api/usage/om-usage`, ya kamata masu kira su lura da bambanci tsakanin
kason da **fitowar rubutu zalla** ke gabatarwa da kuma **fitowar JSON** mai tsararrun bayanai:

| Fanni                 | Tsarin rubutu zalla (`/api/usage/om-usage` / `@@om-usage`)                                                 | Tsarin JSON (`?format=json`)                                                            |
| :-------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Filin fitarwa**     | `NN% left` (misali, `20% left`)                                                                            | `provider.quotas` / `providers[].quotas`                                                |
| **Ma’auni**           | **Izinin amfani da aka daidaita da iyakar yankewa**: ragowar ƙarfi sama da iyakar yankewa                  | **Ɗanyen ƙa’idar upstream**: ma’aunin provider da ba a daidaita ba                      |
| **Daidaita yankewa**  | Ana daidaita shi ta `effectiveRemainingPercent()` domin ajiyar da aka kare ta bayyana a matsayin `0% left` | Babu (ɗanyen ƙimomin hoto kamar yadda provider ya bayar)                                |
| **Bayanan iyaka**     | An haɗa su cikin kason da ake nunawa                                                                       | Ana adana su a `quotaWindowThresholds` na kowace connection (ko tsoffin ƙimomin server) |
| **Babban mai amfani** | CLIs masu mu’amala, umarnin terminal, masu gudanarwa na ɗan Adam                                           | Haɗin shirye-shirye, dashboards, nazari, masu bibiyar kuɗi                              |

#### Tsarin Lissafin Daidaitawa da Iyakar Yankewa

OmniRoute yana goyon bayan yanke ƙa’idar amfani tun da wuri domin hana ƙarewar upstream (wanda ake saita shi ta
`quotaWindowThresholds` na connection, tsoffin ƙimomin provider, ko saitunan juriya na gaba ɗaya). Idan iyakar yankewa
(misali, `cutoff = 25%`) tana aiki a kan taga ta ƙa’idar amfani, mai fitar da rubutu yana lissafa ragowar izinin amfani kamar haka:

- Idan `remaining <= cutoff`: yana mayar da `0%`.
- Idan `remaining > cutoff`: yana mayar da `((remaining - cutoff) / (100 - cutoff)) * 100`.

Fitowar rubutun da aka tsara tana zagaye wannan zuwa cikakkiyar lamba mafi kusa (`Math.round(...)`).

#### Misali Mai Bayani

Yi la’akari da taga ta provider mai **60% ɗanyen amfani** (wato **40% ɗanyen ƙa’idar amfani ya rage**) da kuma
iyakar **25% ta yanke ragowar ƙa’idar amfani** da aka saita:

| Wakilci                          | Lissafi                                            | Ragowar |            Amfani da Aka Fahimta             |
| :------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------- | :-----: | :------------------------------------------: |
| **Ɗanyen JSON (`?format=json`)** | Hoton upstream (`quotas[window]`)                  | **40%** |                   **60%**                    |
| **Tsarin rubutu (`NN% left`)**   | `((40 - 25) / (100 - 25)) * 100 = (15 / 75) * 100` | **20%** | **80%** (idan ana lissafa `100 - remaining`) |

- **Dalilin da rubutu ke nuna ragowar 20%:** Daga cikin 40% na zahirin ƙa’idar amfani da ya rage a account, an ware 25% ta
  manufar yankewa. Mai kiran yana da ragowar 15% ne kawai daga cikin 75% na sararin amfani kafin router ya daina
  tura zirga-zirga zuwa wannan connection. Daidaitawar tana tabbatar da cewa connection zai nuna ƙarewa (`0% left`) daidai lokacin da
  routing zai tsaya.
- **Dalilin da JSON ke kiyaye ɗanyen ƙimomi:** Payload na JSON yana mayar da ainihin hoton provider (`UsageSnapshot`)
  domin kayan aikin waje su sami damar ganin sahihin amfani na upstream da kuma
  `quotaWindowThresholds` na matakin connection ba tare da daidaitawa mai jawo asarar bayanai daga ɓangaren server ba.

#### Jagorar Sauyin Masu Amfani

Idan wani haɗin waje (kamar plugin na edita ko faɗaɗa na wakili) a baya yana ɗebo rubutu daga
endpoint ɗin (`NN% left`) sannan yana ƙididdige kaso da aka yi amfani da shi a matsayin `100 - NN`, ƙaura zuwa `?format=json` yana nufin:

- Karanta ƙimomin JSON kai tsaye zai nuna **ainihin amfani daga tushen sama** (an yi amfani da 60% a misalin da ke sama).
- Idan haɗin yana son kiyaye ma’anar adadin da ake iya amfani da shi ta endpoint ɗin rubutu, zai iya ƙididdige
  tsarin lissafin ragowar da ke aiki a ɓangaren abokin ciniki ta amfani da `quotaWindowThresholds` don sauye-sauyen da aka keɓance ga matakin haɗi.

### Tasirin jinkiri

Samun HIT na cache ta semantika yana bayar da amsa daga cache **ba tare da kira zuwa
upstream ba**, don haka `X-OmniRoute-Response-Latency` da aka bayar yana kusa da sifili
(ba tare da la’akari da ainihin jinkirin upstream ba). Abokan hulɗa masu kula da jinkiri
(gwajin benchmark, sa ido kan p50/p99) ya kamata su duba taken amsa na
`X-OmniRoute-Cache-Latency`:

| Ƙima        | Ma’ana                                                                  |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `synthetic` | An bayar da amsa daga cache; jinkirin ba ainihin lokacin upstream ba ne |
| _(babu)_    | Amsa daga ainihin kiran upstream                                        |

### Tsallake cache bisa kowane maɓalli

Maɓallan API za su iya ƙin karatu daga cache ta semantika ta hanyar `cacheDefaultMode`:

| Ƙima     | Halayya                                                     |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| `legacy` | Halayyar cache ta yau da kullum (tsoho)                     |
| `bypass` | Tsallake binciken cache gaba ɗaya; koyaushe a kira upstream |

Saita yayin ƙirƙirar maɓalli (`POST /api/keys`) ko sabuntawa (`PATCH /api/keys/[id]`):

```json
{ "cacheDefaultMode": "bypass" }
```

### Tsallake cache bisa kowace buƙata

Kowace buƙata za ta iya tsallake cache ba tare da la’akari da saitunan maɓalli ba:

```
X-OmniRoute-No-Cache: true
```

Ga samfuran da aka daidaita/shigo da su, `PUT /api/provider-models` yana karɓar `provider`, `modelId`, da
`maxOutputTokenOverride`: cikakkiyar lamba mai kyau tana saita iyakar output-token da hannu, yayin da `null`
ke share ta don maido da tsohon saiti. `GET /api/provider-models?provider=<provider>` yana dawo da waɗannan
ƙimomi a cikin `modelOutputOverrides`, har da samfuran da ba su da layin samfuri na musamman. Sauyin
yana amfani da damar `max_output_tokens` yayin aiki kuma yana wanzuwa bayan sake daidaita samfuri. Shafin
mai samarwa mai dacewa da OpenAI yana bayar da irin waɗannan hanyoyin gyarawa/sharewa kuma yana yi wa samfuran
da ke da bayyanannen tallafin gani alama.

Nodes na musamman na Chat Completions suna daidaita bayyanannen ƙin amfani da tunani da backend na sama. Gwajin
haɗi da ya yi nasara yana zaɓar sarrafawar chat-template kai tsaye ga kowane ainihin ID na samfuri
wanda shigarwar `/models` ɗinsa ta tabbatar da ƙimar `owned_by` da aka sani: `vllm`, `sglang`, ko `llamacpp`.
Wrappers masu dacewa da OpenAI waɗanda ke aiki ba tare da ɓoye bayanai ba na iya adana asalin shigarwar samfuri
a cikin wani nested object na `openai`; ganowa tana bin har zuwa irin waɗannan envelopes guda uku. Samfuran da
ba su da bayanin mallaka, ko mallakarsu ba a sani ba, ko kuma bayanin ya ci karo, suna ci gaba da halin OpenAI
na yau da kullum. Ganowa tana sake amfani da buƙatar catalog da ake da ita, ba ta samar da completion tokens ba,
kuma tana lalacewa idan mahaɗar haɗin ta sauya.

Don ɗaure wannan hali ga backend wanda ba ya nuna waɗannan metadata, yi amfani da API ɗin sabunta wani ɓangare
na mai samarwa da ake da shi:

```json
{
  "providerSpecificData": {
    "reasoningControl": "chat-template"
  }
}
```

Aika wannan body ɗin tare da `PUT /api/providers/<connection-id>`. A kan wannan haɗin, bayyanannen
ƙoƙarin tunani na `none` ana aika shi a matsayin `chat_template_kwargs.thinking=false` da
`chat_template_kwargs.enable_thinking=false`. Bayyanannun ƙimomin native template suna ci gaba da zama masu
iko sai dai idan dokar tunani ta gefen server ta tilasta wani ƙoƙari. Saitin yana aiki ne kawai lokacin da
haɗin musamman mai dacewa da OpenAI ya tura body na Chat Completions; buƙatun Responses da masu samarwa na
yau da kullum suna riƙe da tsarin buƙatarsu na asali. Saita `reasoningControl` zuwa `openai` don tilasta isar da
`reasoning_effort` na OpenAI na yau da kullum kai tsaye, ko a bar shi/a saita shi zuwa `null` don amfani da ganowa
ta atomatik.

Buƙatun classifier na auto-mode na Claude Code suna sa native thinking ya zama a kashe ta tsohuwa idan ba su
ƙunshi bayyanannun sarrafawar tunani ba. Ganowa tana amfani da alamar system ta classifier a cikin buƙatu masu
tsarin Claude, ba sunayen samfura ko iyakokin completion ba. Bayyanannun sarrafawar body, headers na effort/thinking
da ake tallafawa, dokokin routing, da ƙoƙarin samfuri da aka warware suna riƙe da fifikonsu na yanzu. Duk matakan
classifier biyu suna riƙe da prompts, iyakokin completion, stop sequences, da ainihin hukuncin izini daga upstream;
mataki na biyu har yanzu yana iya samar da bayyanannen tunanin da aka nema a matsayin rubutu na yau da kullum.

### Siga da Tashar Fitarwa

```bash
GET /api/system/version
```

Hanyar gudanarwa ta loopback kawai (tantancewar admin). Tana mayar da sigar da ke gudana, sabuwar
siga da aka wallafa da kuma matsayin sabuntawa ta atomatik. `releaseChannel` da `channels` filaye
ne da aka ƙara (rail 3.8.54); `channel` yana riƙe da ma’anarsa — yanayin turawa da mai sabunta
dashboard ke amfani da shi (`npm`, `source` ko `docker-compose`).

```json
{
  "current": "3.8.52",
  "latest": "3.8.52",
  "updateAvailable": false,
  "channel": "npm",
  "autoUpdateSupported": true,
  "autoUpdateError": null,
  "news": null,
  "releaseChannel": "latest",
  "channels": { "latest": "3.8.52", "next": "3.8.53-rc.1" }
}
```

- `releaseChannel` — tashar npm ta ginin da ke gudana: `nightly` don sigogin `-nightly.*`,
  `next` don sauran sigogin kafin fitarwa (`-rc.*`, `-beta.*`, `-alpha.*`), `lts` don daidaitacciyar siga
  ta tsohuwar babbar siga fiye da `channels.latest`, in ba haka ba `latest`. Dokoki iri ɗaya ne da na
  `scripts/release/dist-tag.mjs`, wanda ke zaɓar npm dist-tag a lokacin wallafawa.
- `channels` — sabuwar sigar da aka wallafa ta kowace dist-tag, daga `npm view omniroute dist-tags`
  (tare da madadin HTTP na rajista), ana adana ta a cache da TTL na mintuna 10 iri ɗaya da na `latest`.
  `latest` yana nan koyaushe (yana komawa ga filin `latest`, sannan `"unavailable"`); `next`, `nightly`
  da `lts` suna bayyana ne kawai idan wannan dist-tag ɗin yana nan. Buƙatar `Cache-Control: no-cache`
  tana sabunta dukkan binciken biyu.

An bayyana tsarin tashoshin (`latest` = v3 har zuwa 4.0 GA, `next` = rc, `nightly` = gine-ginen
`develop`, `lts` = gyare-gyaren v3 bayan 4.0 GA) a cikin `docs/ops/RELEASE_STRATEGY.md`.

## Iyakokin Token

Kasafin **token** na kowane maɓallin API (wanda ya bambanta da Kasafin da ya dogara da USD da ke sama). Ana tilasta shi kai tsaye a hanyar buƙata: idan yawan amfani na taga ta yanzu na wani maɓalli ya kai iyakarsa, ana ƙin buƙatun da `429 Too Many Requests`. Ana iya keɓance iyakoki ga takamaiman `model`, wani `provider`, ko a yi amfani da su `global`ly a duk faɗin maɓallin; idan iyakoki da yawa sun dace da buƙata, mafi tsauri ne zai yi aiki.

```bash
# Jera iyakokin token na wani maɓalli (ya haɗa da amfani na taga kai tsaye)
GET /api/usage/token-limits?apiKeyId=key-123

# Ƙirƙiri ko sabunta iyakar token
POST /api/usage/token-limits
Content-Type: application/json

{
  "apiKeyId": "key-123",
  "scopeType": "model",
  "scopeValue": "openai/gpt-4o",
  "tokenLimit": 1000000,
  "resetInterval": "monthly",
  "enabled": true
}

# Share iyakar token ta amfani da id
DELETE /api/usage/token-limits?id=tl-abc
```

> **Bayanan tsari** (`setTokenLimitSchema`): Ana buƙatar `apiKeyId` da `scopeType` (`model` | `provider` | `global`). Ana buƙatar `scopeValue` sai dai idan `scopeType` ya kasance `global` (misali, id na samfuri don iyakar `model`, ko id na mai bayarwa don iyakar `provider`). Dole ne `tokenLimit` ya zama cikakkiyar lamba mai kyau (ana sauya shi daga kirtani). Na zaɓi: `id` (a bar shi don ƙirƙira, a bayar da shi don sabuntawa), `resetInterval` (`daily` | `weekly` | `monthly`, tsoho `monthly`), `resetTime` (`HH:MM`), `enabled` (tsoho `true`). Amsoshin `GET` suna ƙara wa kowace iyaka bayanan `tokensUsed`, `remaining`, `windowStart`, `periodStartAt`, da `nextResetAt`. Wannan ƙarshen hanya ne na ajin gudanarwa (ana tilasta tantance izini a tsakiya ta bututun authz).

## Sarrafa Buƙata

1. Abokin ciniki yana aika buƙata zuwa `/v1/*`
2. Mai sarrafa hanya yana kiran `handleChat`, `handleEmbedding`, `handleAudioTranscription`, ko `handleImageGeneration`
3. Ana tantance samfuri (mai bayarwa/samfuri kai tsaye ko alias/combo)
4. Ana zaɓar bayanan shaida daga DB na gida tare da tace samuwar asusu
5. Don hira: `handleChatCore` yana bincika ma'ajiyar semantic/signature kuma yana tantance saitunan matsawar combo
6. Matsawa ta riga-kafi tana gudana kafin fassarar mai bayarwa idan an kunna ta (`lite`, Caveman, RTK, ko waɗanda aka jera tare)
7. Mai aiwatar da mai bayarwa yana aika buƙatar zuwa sama
8. Ana sake fassara amsa zuwa tsarin abokin ciniki (hira) ko a mayar da ita yadda take (embeddings/hotuna/sauti)
9. Ana yin rikodin amfani, nazarin matsawa, da rajistan buƙatu
10. Ana amfani da hanyar madadin idan an samu kurakurai bisa ga ƙa'idojin combo

Cikakken bayani kan gine-ginen tsarin: [`ARCHITECTURE.md`](../architecture/ARCHITECTURE.md)

## Juriya (faɗaɗa)

OmniRoute yana samar da hanyoyi uku masu zaman kansu na sarrafa gazawar wucin gadi; maƙarƙashiyoyin gudanarwa da ke ƙasa suna ba masu gudanarwa damar karantawa da sauya su:

| Iyaka                 | Ma'ajiyar yanayi                             | Karantawa                                 | Sake saiti / sharewa                                              |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mai katse mai samarwa | `domain_circuit_breakers` + ƙwaƙwalwar ciki  | `/api/monitoring/health`                  | `POST /api/resilience/reset`                                      |
| Lokacin jiran haɗi    | `rateLimitedUntil` a haɗe-haɗen masu samarwa | `/api/rate-limits`, `/api/providers/[id]` | (yana sake kunnawa a hankali; share ta hanyar PUT na mai samarwa) |
| Kullewar samfuri      | Rijistar samuwar samfuri a ƙwaƙwalwar ciki   | `GET /api/resilience/model-cooldowns`     | `DELETE /api/resilience/model-cooldowns`                          |

`PATCH /api/resilience` yana karɓar sauye-sauyen mai katse mai samarwa ƙarƙashin `providerBreaker.oauth` da `providerBreaker.apikey`. Kowane bayanin martaba yana goyon bayan `degradationThreshold`, `failureThreshold`, da `resetTimeoutMs`; ana kuma nuna filayen iri ɗaya a Allon Sarrafawa → Saituna → Juriya.

```bash
# Share kullewar samfuri guda ɗaya
curl -X DELETE http://localhost:20128/api/resilience/model-cooldowns \
  -H "Cookie: auth_token=..." \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"provider":"openai","model":"gpt-4o-mini"}'

# Goge dukkan kulle-kullen
curl -X DELETE http://localhost:20128/api/resilience/model-cooldowns \
  -H "Cookie: auth_token=..." \
  -d '{"all":true}'
```

Don cikakken bayanin ra'ayi da tsoffin saitunan mai katsewa: duba [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) → "Yanayin Juriya na Lokacin Gudanarwa" da [`docs/architecture/RESILIENCE_GUIDE.md`](../architecture/RESILIENCE_GUIDE.md).

### Layin Jiran Iyakance Ƙima & Kariyar Kunna Kai-tsaye

- **Hanyar rubutawa da ake goyon baya**: `POST /api/rate-limits` tare da jiki `{"connectionId": "<id>", "enabled": true|false}` shi ne maƙarƙashiyar gudanarwa da ake goyon baya don kunna ko kashe kariyar iyakance ƙima ga wani haɗi. Da gangan `PUT /api/providers/[id]` ba ya gyara ko sauya `rateLimitProtection` (#11278).
- **Ma'anar `enabled: false`**: Saita `enabled: false` yana cire kariya da aka ayyana kai tsaye ga haɗin, amma **ba ya** cire haɗin mabuɗin API mai aiki daga kariyar kunna kai-tsaye ta duniya (`autoEnableApiKeyProviders = true`). A cikin `reconcileEnabledConnections` (`open-sse/services/rateLimitManager.ts`), `false` da `undefined` suna da ma'ana ɗaya; haɗe-haɗen mabuɗin API masu aiki waɗanda ba su da kariya da aka ayyana kai tsaye za su ci gaba da samun kariya ƙarƙashin kariyar kunna kai-tsaye.
- **Kashewa a duniya**: Don gudanar da haɗe-haɗen mabuɗin API ba tare da layukan jiran iyakance ƙima ba, kashe kariyar a duniya ta hanyar `RATE_LIMIT_AUTO_ENABLE=false` (muhalli) ko `resilienceSettings.requestQueue.autoEnableApiKeyProviders = false` (Saituna → Juriya / `PATCH /api/resilience`).

OmniRoute na zuwa da uwar garken Model Context Protocol da aka haɗa a ciki, mai hanyoyin sufuri 3 (stdio, SSE, streamable-http) da kayan aiki masu iyakokin izini. Ƙofofin dashboard da ke ƙasa suna karanta bayanan matsayi/binciken aiki kuma suna wakiltar hanyoyin sufuri na HTTP.

| Hanya | Tafarki | Bayani |
| ------ | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------- |
| GET | `/api/mcp/status` | Siginar rai, hanyar sufuri, matsayin kan layi, kira na ƙarshe, manyan kayan aiki, ƙimar nasara ta awanni 24 |
| GET | `/api/mcp/tools` | Jerin kayan aikin MCP tare da `name`, `description`, `scopes`, `phase`, `auditLevel`, `sourceEndpoints` |
| GET | `/api/mcp/sse` | Buɗe rafin SSE don hanyar sufurin SSE (yana mayar da `503` idan an kashe MCP ko hanyar sufuri ba ta dace ba) |
| POST | `/api/mcp/sse` | Aika firam ɗin JSON-RPC ta hanyar sufurin SSE |
| GET | `/api/mcp/stream` | Buɗe ɓangaren SSE na hanyar sufurin Streamable HTTP (saƙonnin da uwar garken ta fara aikawa) |
| POST | `/api/mcp/stream` | Aika firam ɗin JSON-RPC ta hanyar sufurin Streamable HTTP |
| DELETE | `/api/mcp/stream` | Ƙare zaman Streamable HTTP |
| GET | `/api/mcp/audit` | Tambayi rajistar binciken aiki — `?limit=`, `?offset=`, `?tool=`, `?success=true                                | false`, `?apiKeyId=` |
| GET | `/api/mcp/audit/stats` | Ƙididdigar binciken aiki da aka tattara (jimloli, ƙimar nasara, matsakaicin tsawon lokaci, manyan kayan aiki) |

**Tabbatar da izini:** hanyoyin sufurin `sse`/`stream` suna bin tsarin tabbatar da izini na musamman ga MCP (maɓallin API na Bearer mai izinin `mcp`); ana iya karanta hanyoyin `status`/`tools`/`audit*` daga dashboard (ba a buƙatar ƙarin tabbaci bayan samun damar isa ga mai masaukin dashboard).

> Dukkan hanyoyin sufurin HTTP suna ƙarƙashin ikon `settings.mcpEnabled` da `settings.mcpTransport` — rashin dacewar hanyar sufuri yana mayar da `400`, yayin da kashe MCP ke mayar da `503`.

## Sabar A2A

OmniRoute yana samar da maƙurar A2A (Agent-to-Agent) ta JSON-RPC 2.0 tare da marufin REST don dubawa/amfani da dashboard.

### JSON-RPC

```bash
POST /a2a
Authorization: Bearer your-api-key   # na zaɓi ne sai dai idan an saita OMNIROUTE_API_KEY
Content-Type: application/json

{
  "jsonrpc": "2.0",
  "id": 1,
  "method": "message/send",
  "params": {
    "skill": "smart-routing",
    "messages": [{"role": "user", "content": "Route this coding task"}]
  }
}
```

Hanyoyin da ake tallafawa (duk suna ƙarƙashin kunna `settings.a2aEnabled`):

| Hanya            | Bayani                                                                   |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `message/send`   | Aiwatar da ƙwarewa kai tsaye; yana dawo da `{task, artifacts, metadata}` |
| `message/stream` | Aiwatar da SSE mai gudana na wannan rukunin ƙwarewar                     |
| `tasks/get`      | Ɗauko aiki ta amfani da `taskId`                                         |
| `tasks/cancel`   | Soke aiki ta amfani da `taskId`                                          |

Ƙwarewar da aka tanada: `smart-routing`, `quota-management`, `provider-discovery`, `cost-analysis`, `health-report`.

### Katin Wakili

```bash
GET /.well-known/agent.json
```

Yana dawo da katin wakilin A2A na jama'a (suna, bayani, iyawa, kundin ƙwarewa, tsarin tantancewa) — ana adana shi a cache na jama'a na tsawon 1h. Ba a buƙatar tantancewa.

### Mataimakan REST

| Hanya | Tafarki                      | Bayani                                                                                                           |
| ----- | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| GET   | `/api/a2a/status`            | Kunna A2A + ƙididdigar ayyuka + taƙaitaccen katin wakili da aka adana a cache                                    |
| GET   | `/api/a2a/tasks`             | Jera ayyuka — `?state=submitted\|working\|completed\|failed\|cancelled`, `?skill=`, `?limit=` (≤200), `?offset=` |
| POST  | `/api/a2a/tasks`             | (Ba a aiwatar da shi a matsayin mataimakin REST ba — ƙirƙira ta JSON-RPC `message/send`)                         |
| GET   | `/api/a2a/tasks/[id]`        | Maido da aiki guda ɗaya                                                                                          |
| POST  | `/api/a2a/tasks/[id]/cancel` | Soke aiki                                                                                                        |

**Tantancewa:** mataimakan REST suna aiki ba tare da tantancewar gudanarwa ba (dashboard na iya karantawa); hanyar JSON-RPC `/a2a` tana amfani da Bearer `OMNIROUTE_API_KEY` idan an saita shi.

## Tantancewa

Duba [Tantancewar Gudanarwa](../guides/MANAGEMENT-AUTH.md) don rukunoni huɗu na bayanan shaida
(zaman dashboard, token na CLI na gida, Token Samun Dama na `oma_live_…`,
maɓallin API mai iyakar izinin gudanarwa) da yadda suka bambanta da maɓallan inference.

- Hanyoyin dashboard (`/dashboard/*`) suna amfani da kuki na `auth_token`
- Shiga yana amfani da hash ɗin kalmar sirri da aka adana; idan bai samu ba, sai a koma ga `INITIAL_PASSWORD`
- Ana iya kunna ko kashe `requireLogin` ta hanyar `/api/settings/require-login`
- Hanyoyin `/v1/*` na iya buƙatar maɓallin API na Bearer idan `REQUIRE_API_KEY=true`
- “token na gudanarwa” / “maɓallin API mai iyakar izinin gudanarwa” a cikin wannan jagora yana nufin ɗaya daga cikin rukunonin da ke cikin wancan jagorar — ba wani ƙarin nau'in sirri da ba a bayyana ba

> **Sauyin da zai karya dacewa (v3.8.0)** — `/api/v1/agents/tasks/*` da wuraren ƙarshen gudanar da lokacin dakatawa yanzu suna buƙatar **tantancewar gudanarwa** (kukin `auth_token` na dashboard ko maɓallin API mai iyakar izinin gudanarwa). Abokan cinikin da a baya suke kiran waɗannan hanyoyin ba tare da tantancewa ba za su karɓi `401 Unauthorized`. Duba ƙaddamarwar `588a0333` (`fix(auth): require management auth for agent and cooldown APIs`).
